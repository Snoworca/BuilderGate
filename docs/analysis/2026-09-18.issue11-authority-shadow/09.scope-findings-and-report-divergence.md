# Issue #11 — scope findings left deliberately, and a disagreement between the issue's own reports

## 1. Two behaviours are absent. They were left absent on purpose.

Recorded so a later reader sees why they were **left** rather than **missed**.

### (a) Issue AC-5's convergence mechanism

> …bounded ordered chunk/digest 또는 observable rejection+fresh resync로 수렴한다.

The first half of AC-5 holds structurally (§4). The convergence half has **no implementation
and no configuration**: `checkpointChunk` reports `configured: false, value: null`, asserted
by both `REL-BGSTAB-011` AC-3 and `REL-BGSTAB-007` AC-6.

**Not implemented here because:**

1. **No SRS criterion requires it.** The AC map (§3, 913 ACs across 135 requirements, four
   controls) found no criterion asking for chunked or digest-verified convergence at this
   layer. Building it means **authoring a requirement**, which is exactly the near-miss the
   standing brief records against #10 — a new requirement duplicating live stable ones.
2. **`checkpointChunk` is a config key.** Adding one carries the full `REL-BGSTAB-023`
   procedure plus a `REL-BGSTAB-007` **AC-11 approval gate**, which the orchestrator has
   explicitly **not granted and is not granting** (`#101`). AC-11 says in terms that this
   requirement "resource profile/default 숫자 변경"을 자동 승인하지 않는다.
3. `REL-BGSTAB-007` AC-6 states outright: *"이 Requirement는 새 aggregate/chunk/inflight
   숫자를 승인하지 않는다."*

### (b) Issue AC-4's current-screen comparison arm

> 기존 viewport snapshot의 current screen과 새 checkpoint를 비교하고, **그리고**
> 새 checkpoint는 pre-refresh model의 full retained … parity를 shadow 비교한다.

Two comparisons. Only the second exists. `REL-BGSTAB-011` AC-4 specifies the eight-axis
full-retained comparison and has **no current-screen-vs-checkpoint arm** — divergence (a)
in §3.

**Not implemented here for reason 1 above**: no SRS criterion asks for it, so building it is
authoring a requirement.

**Both are recorded as scope findings, not as defects.** The behaviour is absent from the
code *and* from the specification, consistently. That is a smaller claim than "this is
broken" and it is the one the measurement supports.

## 2. The issue's three completion reports disagree with each other

Issue #11 carries **three near-identical PH-004 completion reports**. Their §7 seal tables
are not identical:

| Artifact | Earlier report | Later two reports | Matches this tree? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `retained-shadow-parity.json` | `b4eefd4d378b174cf12139c281147d232b37d815f25aa7c86092b7c6befe5c0b` | `9914e22418e315184ef7cdc315b95b57d693efbe6ad6f84da144ac59a248e265` | **only the later value** |

Every other row is identical across all three copies.

The later report states it supersedes the earlier one — *"GitHub #11 최초 구현 보고
`issuecomment-4999195944`는 pre-closure artifact hash를 담고 있어 … `issuecomment-4999298118`이
supersede한다"* — so §1 used the superseding value and said so. Against the earlier value the
manifest count would have been **6 of 8 mismatching rather than 5 of 8**.

**Why this is a finding rather than bookkeeping.** Two sealed manifests for the same file,
differing in one row, means **at least one of those reports was written against a tree its
own manifest does not describe.** A seal certifies that bytes have not changed since
sealing; it says nothing about whether they were correct when sealed. Here the artifact was
resealed between copies while the surrounding prose — including its "No findings" verdicts
and its `57/57`, `42축`, `517/517`, `485/485`, `5/5` numbers — was carried over **unchanged**.
So the prose of at least one copy is attached to a hash it was not measured with.

That is the same class as this bundle's §4 finding that the report's `517/517` is `538
PASS / 3 FAIL` here: **the numbers in these reports travel independently of the trees they
describe.**

## 3. The bound on all of the above

§1's conclusion is unchanged and is not to be rounded off by any later summary: the manifest
mismatch establishes **only** that none of the PH-004 result claims may be inherited here,
