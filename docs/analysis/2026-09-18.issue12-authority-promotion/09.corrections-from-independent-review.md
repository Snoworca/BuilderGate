# Issue #12 — two corrections to this bundle, found by independent analysts

HEAD `737f190d92e2ecf2154100ce313ae10295cd72da`. Written during the `/kiwi-srs` step, when three
isolated Phase-1 analysts re-derived this bundle's claims from the tree. Per the

## Correction 1 — `04`/`07` overstate how the responder hazard is excluded

**What I wrote** (`04.ac2-zero-window.md`, Step 2):

> Both conjunctions include `responder.active`, which holds **one** value. … **The hazard is
> excluded by a single-valued discriminator, not by the pair of booleans the AC names.**

**What the independent code analyst measured:** the two guards check **five** and **eight**
conjuncts respectively, and `serverEnabled` / `legacyEnabled` are among them. Its verdict on my
claim as phrased was **refuted**, with the warning that anyone relying on my sentence to justify
removing or ignoring the booleans would break both guard functions.

**Adjudication — the analyst is right about the prose and I was right about the logic, and the
prose is what ships.** The entailment I stated is sound: `responder.active` appearing in both
conjunctions is *sufficient* to exclude simultaneous acceptance, whatever the booleans do. But
"**not** by the pair of booleans" is a claim about what is load-bearing, and that is false — the
booleans are read and enforced on the real path.

Worse, **my own mutation data already contradicted the singular attribution** and I did not carry
it back into the sentence. M2 (delete `responder.active` alone) **survived**, because
`admission.mode` closes the path by itself; M3 needed a group deletion. So the exclusion is
**over-determined by at least three conjuncts** — `admission.mode`, `responder.active` and
`activeLeaseId` — and naming one of them as *the* mechanism was never supported, not even by my
own table on the next page.

This is the standing brief's *"the bias can live in the write-up after you have fixed it in the
instrument"*, and it is the second time in this lane: I measured correctly and then narrated a
stronger claim than the measurement licensed.

**The corrected statement:** *simultaneous admission on the two query-reply paths is excluded
several times over. At least three conjuncts each suffice independently, and one of them —
`responder.active` — is single-valued, which is why no ordering of the booleans can produce a
duplicate. The criterion names only the booleans.* The last sentence is the finding; the rest is
the mechanism, and it is plural.

## Correction 2 — `01`'s AC-11 map missed the corpus's only `dedup ledger` text

**What I wrote** (`01.ac-map.md`, divergence (d)): that the ledger-retention clause is absent
everywhere, measured as `dedup ledger` 0 hits, `epoch 안정` 0, `안정될 때까지` 0.

**What the independent SRS analyst found:** `REL-BGSTAB-011` **AC-9** — checked — is the only
criterion in all 913 rows that names the **`fact/dedup/checkpoint ledger`**:

> PTY natural exit, explicit stop/delete, session generation replacement와 terminal close는 model
> admission을 먼저 닫고 driver lease를 revoke한 뒤 **fact/dedup/checkpoint ledger**와 timers를
> 정확히 한 번 정산해야 하며 …

**My probe could not see it.** I searched for the phrase `dedup ledger`; the corpus spells it
`fact/dedup/checkpoint ledger`. My own control table shows `dedup` returning **2 hits across 2
requirements**, one of them `REL-BGSTAB-011` — **the control fired and I did not follow it.** The
enumeration was narrower than the claim, which is the diagnosis `#10` arrived at after five
same-direction corrections, and it is mine now too.

**Does the conclusion survive?** Yes, and it is worth stating why rather than asserting it. AC-9
governs **session termination** — PTY exit, stop/delete, generation replacement, close — and
requires the ledger be *settled exactly once* there. It says nothing about an authority
**rollback**, and nothing about what retaining the ledger would be *for*. So issue AC-11's clause
is still unanchored. But the honest form of the finding changed:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| claim | no criterion mentions the dedup ledger at all | **one does** — `REL-BGSTAB-011` AC-9 |
| basis | a phrase search returning zero | a phrase search returning zero **that I should have widened** |
| what is actually missing | the whole subject | the ledger's behaviour **at rollback**, and the purpose of retaining it |

The second row is the better finding: a criterion that settles the ledger at termination and is
silent at rollback is a **sharper** gap than no criterion at all, because it shows the ledger's
lifecycle was specified for one boundary and not the other.

**Method note for the next reader:** a substring probe over AC text finds the spelling you
thought of. Two of my three probes for this clause were multi-word phrases, which is the most
brittle shape a substring search can take. Where a concept may appear inside a compound, probe
the **single most distinctive token** and read every hit — `dedup`, not `dedup ledger`.
