## Result handling

Await the observer's natural close and output completion. Its onDeadline callback records the118000ms failure through the existing diagnostic port without throwing deliberately to cancel work. The observer already retains notification errors and continues waiting. No callback or assertion may report completion before observer settlement.

After settlement, record elapsed/code/signal and preserve full stdout/stderr in failure diagnostics. Fail on spawnError, any observationErrors, nonzero code, non-null signal, deadlineExceeded, or elapsedMs>=118000. Do not treat empty stderr as proof of complete output or nonempty stderr alone as a failure: expected child diagnostics are reporter output records; process failures remain independently checked.

Decode exact LF-terminated stdout once; JSON framing/syntax errors propagate, without trimming/BOM stripping/fallback. Evaluate against the exact absolute entry paths derived from workspaceRoot plus fixedClosureTests. Require accepted=true and report all reasons on failure. The existing validator owns8-counter reconciliation, per-entry nonempty success, exactly one root summary/end, skip/TODO/cancellation rejection and output-text isolation. Never count complete events again or use definition-site event.file as entry attribution.

One invocation produces one run result. Three fresh complete runs on the same approved tree/input contract remain required for admission completion; do not implement hidden retries until one succeeds or label prior selected-parent evidence as a full run.
